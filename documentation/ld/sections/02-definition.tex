\section{Performance measurement framework (CIGRE TB 956)}

CIGRE Technical Brochure~956 (2025), which supersedes TB~590, defines a 
standardized protocol for reporting the annual operational performance of HVDC
systems. The reporting period is the calendar year, and only events causing a loss
of transfer capacity are reported. These definitions are the basis on which
availability and realibility guarantees --- and hence liquidated damages --- are written.

\subsection{Outage classification}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0.2em}
  \item \textbf{Scheduled outage} --- planned or deferrable (e.g.\ preventive
        maintenance).
  \item \textbf{Forced outage} --- equipment unavailable outside a scheduled
        state, whether by protective trip or other forced derating.
  \item \textbf{Forced Outage Rate (FOR)} --- number of complete or partial
        forced outages of a converter (or pole) per unit of operating time.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Capacity and duration}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0.2em}
  \item \textbf{Rated capacity} $P_m$ --- maximum continuous capacity (MW),
        excluding redundant equipment.
  \item \textbf{Outage capacity} $P_o$ --- the capacity reduction that an outage would have caused if the HVDC system was operating at $P_m$.
  \item \textbf{Outage Derating Factor} $\mathrm{ODF}=\dfrac{P_o}{P_m}$ ---
        1 for a full outage, between 0 and 1 for a partial loss.
  \item \textbf{Equivalent Outage Duration} $\mathrm{EOD}=\mathrm{AOD}\times
        \mathrm{ODF}$ --- actual outage duration weighted by the derating.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Availability and unavailability}
With period hours $\mathrm{PH}$ (8760\,h/yr) and equivalent outage hours
$\mathrm{EOH}=\sum\mathrm{EOD}$:
\[
  \mathrm{EU}\,(\%) = \frac{\mathrm{EOH}}{\mathrm{PH}}\times 100,
  \qquad
  \mathrm{EA}\,(\%) = 100 - \mathrm{EU}.
\]
Unavailability is split by cause into \textbf{Forced} (FEU) and \textbf{Scheduled}
(SEU) energy unavailability:
\[
  \mathrm{FEU}=\frac{\mathrm{EFOH}}{\mathrm{PH}}\times 100,
  \qquad
  \mathrm{SEU}=\frac{\mathrm{ESOH}}{\mathrm{PH}}\times 100.
\]
FEU, SEU and FOR are the metrics against which performance guarantees are
typically written.
