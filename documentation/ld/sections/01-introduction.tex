\section{Introduction and scope}

\subsection{Background}
American Terawatt's HVDC schemes are being developed to supply electric power to large industrial customers, 
including data-center loads. For such customers, the value of the HVDC connection depends
not only on its rated power supply capacity but also on how reliably it can deliver the energy over time.
Availability and reliability performance of the HVDC converter stations, therefore  
shall be contractual guarantees in future EPC contracts for the HVDC converter 
stations, and  the typical remedy in HVDC contracts for a shortfall against 
availability and reliability performance guarantees is liquidated damages.

\subsection{Objective}
This report sets out:
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0.15em}
  \item[(i)] the performance framework used to measure HVDC availability;
  \item[(ii)] the values that can realistically be guaranteed;
  \item[(iii)] how liquidated damages are structured against those guarantees;
        and
  \item[(iv)] a recommended strategy for American Terawatt when negotiating LDs
        with an equipment supplier (OEM).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Scope}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{In scope:} availability- and reliability-based liquidated
        damages for the HVDC transmission system (converter stations and DC
        line), assessed under CIGRE TB~956.
  \item \textbf{Out of scope:} construction delay damages, defect liability, and
        the detailed PPA terms with the end customer (referenced only where they
        inform the LD basis).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Definitions and abbreviations}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0.15em}
  \item \textbf{LD} --- Liquidated Damages.
  \item \textbf{OEM} --- Original Equipment Manufacturer (HVDC converter station supplier).
  \item \textbf{PPA} --- Power Purchase Agreement (with the end customer).
  \item \textbf{EA / EU} --- Energy Availability / Unavailability (\%).
  \item \textbf{FEU / SEU} --- Forced / Scheduled Energy Unavailability (\%).
  \item \textbf{FOR} --- Forced Outage Rate (outages per year).
  \item \textbf{ODF} --- Outage Derating Factor; \textbf{EOD/EOH} --- Equivalent
        Outage Duration / Hours.
\end{itemize}
