\subsection{Single-phase transformer 240$\times$480\,V -- 120/240\,V}
\label{sec:na-single}

The single-phase unit has two primary sections of 240\,V and two secondary
sections of 120\,V, Figure~\ref{fig:na-single}. For a 480\,V supply the
primary sections are connected in series by a jumper between H2 and H3; for
a 240\,V supply they are connected in parallel, H1 to H3 and H2 to H4. The
secondary sections are connected in series, and the joint X2--X3 is the
neutral, grounded as the system grounded conductor. The secondary then
supplies 120\,V from either line to neutral and 240\,V between the lines.

Three such units form a three-phase bank with any of the connections of
Sections~\ref{sec:na-delta} and~\ref{sec:na-wye}; two units form the open
delta of Section~\ref{sec:na-open}.
