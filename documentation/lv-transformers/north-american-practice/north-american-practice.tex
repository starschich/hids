\section{480\,V to 240\,V Connections in North America}
\label{sec:na}

North American commercial and industrial buildings are supplied at
480Y/277\,V or 480\,V delta. Motors take 480\,V and lighting 277\,V; a
dry-type transformer inside the building derives the lower utilization
voltage, 240\,V three-phase, 120/240\,V single-phase or 208Y/120\,V.

Section~\ref{sec:na-terms} gives the terminal markings, the phase shift and
the grounding of the derived system. Sections~\ref{sec:na-single}
to~\ref{sec:na-open} describe the connections: the single-phase unit, the
three-phase connections with a delta and with a wye winding, and the open
delta. Section~\ref{sec:na-application} lists the checks common to all of
them, and Section~\ref{sec:na-summary} compares the connections.
