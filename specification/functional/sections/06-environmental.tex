\section{Environmental and site conditions}
\label{sec:environmental}

The converter stations are located in Utah and Nevada. The environmental and
site design conditions to be used for the design and rating of all equipment
are given in Table~\ref{tab:environmental-conditions}. 

Both sites are greenfield sites. No pre-existing structures, foundations,
buried services or adjacent installations constrain the layout, and no
restriction is placed on the station footprint or equipment arrangement. The supplier is therefore free to
propose the station layout that gives the best overall techno-economic result.
The final site boundaries, access roads and grading will be provided during
the design phase; the supplier shall state the footprint and the maximum equipment weight to be brought on site.
