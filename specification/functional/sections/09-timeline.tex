\section{Timeline}
\label{sec:timeline}

The preliminray milestones are listed in Table~\ref{tab:timeline-milestones}. The supplier should indicate the 
anticipated NTP date required to meet the presented timeline.

The timeline assumes that the 3000 MW systems are built out in two steps, with a time gap of more than
one year between the two steps. For the DSMP configuration, one of the two symmetrical monopoles is
built first and the second monopole follows more than one year later. For the BIP configuration, the first pole
is built first and the second pole follows later.

With this staged buildout, the four connections presented in Section~\ref{sec:unig} result in six link configurations. The
NTP and COD milestones in Table~\ref{tab:timeline-milestones} are therefore given per link configuration rather than per connection.
