\section{AC data}
\label{sec:ac-data}
The AC system data is flexible and is not fixed at the time of issue of this
specification. A nominal AC voltage of 230\,kV has been selected as a compromise
between the insulation requirements, which grow with the system voltage
(clearances, creepage, BIL and the resulting station and tower footprint), and
the current requirements, which grow as the voltage is reduced (conductor and
busbar sizing, switchgear and transformer current ratings, and losses). The
supplier is encouraged to propose an alternative AC voltage where this results
in a significant cost or performance benefit.

The interconnection requirements follow IEEE~2800. The steady-state parameters
at the point of measurement (POM) are given in
Table~\ref{tab:ac-steady-state}. The insulation coordination for the 230\,kV
system follows IEEE~1313.1 and IEEE~C57.12.00 and is given in
Table~\ref{tab:ac-insulation}.
