\section{AC data}
\label{sec:ac-data}
The nominal AC voltage for the generation and load side are 230\,kV. Although not connected to the AC grid, the interconnection requirements should follow the requirements defined in IEEE~2800 and \mbox{PRC-029-2}.
The AC system data are collected in Table~\ref{tab:ac-data}, covering the
steady-state and transient voltage levels, the frequency range and the
short-circuit levels. The temporary voltage range is shown separately in
Figure~\ref{fig:ac-frt}.

Concept verification studies are ongoing. The voltage and frequency range will be adapted to take into account the islanded
network configuration.

The continuous frequency range corresponds to the ``no trip'' range of
\mbox{PRC-029-2}, within which the converter shall remain connected and in continuous
operation at rated power without any restriction. Outside this range, the
frequency ride-through capability shall follow the duration-dependent
requirements of \mbox{PRC-029-2}. As noted above, the range will be reviewed once the
concept verification studies for the islanded network configuration are
completed.

The short-circuit levels apply at the AC terminals of each station and are
subject to confirmation by the ongoing concept verification studies.
