\section{Standards and compliance}

The system shall comply with the latest editions of the applicable standards,
including (indicative):

\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0.15em}
  \item IEC 60633 --- Terminology for HVDC transmission.
  \item IEC 62747 --- Terminology for VSC for HVDC systems.
  \item IEC TR 62672 --- Reliability and availability of HVDC systems.
  \item IEC 60071 --- Insulation coordination.
  \item CIGRE TB~956 --- Protocol for reporting operational performance of HVDC
        systems.
  \item CLC/TS 50654 --- HVDC systems and connected converter stations.
  \item The applicable national grid code and interconnection requirements.
\end{itemize}
